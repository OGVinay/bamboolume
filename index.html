Solar Lantern FAQ & Help

How do lanterns charge on cloudy or rainy days?

Our monocrystalline silicon cells feature 22.4% conversion efficiency, allowing them to absorb ambient diffuse UV light even through cloud cover. You'll still achieve 8–10 hours of runtime during overcast conditions.

Are the internal lithium batteries replaceable?

Yes! Every Bamboolume lantern utilizes standardized high-cycle LiFePO4 (Lithium Iron Phosphate) cells rated for 2,500+ charge cycles (over 6 years of daily night use). The battery compartment is easily accessed with a standard screwdriver.

What does the IP65 Marine rating mean?

IP65 guarantees 100% dust-tight sealing and full protection against pressurized water jets from any direction. You never need to bring your lanterns indoors during summer thunderstorms, winter blizzards, or automatic lawn sprinkler cycles.

How does PayPal Buyer Protection protect my order?

Every transaction made via PayPal or PayPal Pay in 4 is backed by PayPal's Buyer Protection policy. If your handcrafted lanterns arrive damaged or fail to meet expectations, you are fully reimbursed immediately.
